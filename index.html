Our Story

Distance should never be the reason someone feels forgotten.

I'm Anu. I grew up watching what miles do to people who love each other — birthdays spent on a video call, big news celebrated alone, apologies typed out instead of held.

So I started making things by hand in my San Jose kitchen. Chocolates with the exact flavour someone remembers from childhood. Roses in her favourite colour. Balloons with a name written in gold. Letters in ink, because a screenshot has never made anyone cry.

Every surprise leaves this home with one job: make someone feel unforgettable.

How It Works

A conversation, not a checkout.

1

Tell us the story

Message us on Instagram or WhatsApp. Who is it for, what do they love, what is the occasion, and what feels comfortable for your budget.

2

We design & quote

Because every surprise is different, there are no fixed prices. We send you options and an honest quote — and yes, we talk it through together.

3

We craft & deliver

Made fresh in our San Jose home, packed by hand, and delivered on the date that matters. Rush timing available — just ask.

Why we quote instead of listing prices: a box of twelve truffles and a full proposal setup are not the same gift. We would rather ask you what you had in mind than force your love story into a price tier.
